# Türkiye social economy pilot: measurement protocol v0.1

Research design dated 4 November 2026. No pilot observations or official scores have been produced. The social economy indicators do not enter the synthetic explorer or any composite calculation.

## Purpose and placement

The pilot examines social and solidarity economy (SSE) employment, organisational resilience and participation. SSE-01 and SSE-02 sit under Sustainability. SSE-03 belongs to Equality and inclusion if eventually admitted to the index. SSE-04 is a thematic research measure. Every indicator has only one scoring home; cross-references do not create additional weight.

## Operational perimeter

Use the [ILO conceptual definition](https://www.ilo.org/topics-and-sectors/social-and-solidarity-economy) as a starting point. The following screening procedure is a proposed CAHSPAR research design, not an ILO-certified classification:

1. Identify an economically active organisation with a stable research identifier and reference period. Legal registration alone does not establish activity.
2. Document collective/general-interest purpose and how that purpose operates in practice.
3. Document autonomy, participatory governance and how surplus/assets are used in relation to the purpose.
4. Classify eligibility as confirmed, excluded or unresolved using published rules and an evidence log. Do not treat an unresolved case as an excluded organisation or zero employment.
5. Track parent entities, branches and affiliated economic enterprises separately, then resolve overlaps before aggregation. Do not add the same workplace twice through a foundation and its enterprise.

Cooperative, association, foundation and company registers are candidate frames, not automatic SSE classifications. Social enterprises may span legal forms. Pilot eligibility rules and evidence thresholds require research-team review before collection.

## Indicator specifications

The downloadable `social-economy-indicators.csv` records four candidates, their numerators, denominators, units, proposed calculations, sources and outstanding gaps.

- SSE-01: annual average paid employee jobs in eligible entities / annual average paid employee jobs in the same domestic economy × 100. A job-based numerator must not be divided by an unadjusted resident-person Labour Force Survey denominator. If person-based measurement is adopted instead, both terms must use the same person, residence, age and reference-period definitions. Members, volunteers and beneficiaries are separate populations. This is a measure of sector size, not causal impact or job quality; no automatic higher-is-better score is assigned.
- SSE-02: eligible social enterprise birth cohort still economically active after three years / complete original eligible birth cohort × 100. Define births, mergers, cessation and eligibility changes. Unknown follow-up is not a closure; incomplete follow-up prevents a headline cohort survival estimate unless a reviewed statistical method is adopted.
- SSE-03: paid entrants from each predefined target group retained at an eligible organisation after six months / all entrants in that group and entry cohort with a full six-month observation window × 100. Publish group-specific results and unknown follow-up separately. This is descriptive retention, not proof of additional employment caused by the organisation. No sensitive person-level records belong in the public repository.
- SSE-04: paid workers reporting a specified participation mechanism and its use during the reference year / valid respondents to the same survey item × 100. Use population weights only with a defensible probability design. Otherwise label results as respondent-only; disclose unit and item non-response.

No candidate has an agreed composite weight, normalisation bound or validated observation yet. A zero denominator produces an undefined value, never zero. Missing source coverage must not be silently dropped and the remainder relabelled as a national total.

## Source review and collection sequence

The source register records evidence and limits, not access promises. The [Ministry of Trade page](https://ticaret.gov.tr/kooperatifcilik/bilgi-bankasi/illere-gore-kooperatif-dagilimi) illustrates why page dates cannot replace observation dates: its displayed table is labelled June 2019 despite a 2026 page date. The [Civil Society Relations portal](https://www.siviltoplum.gov.tr/) identifies association statistics, but a usable paid-employment extract has not been verified. The [Foundations Directorate index](https://www.vgm.gov.tr/vakif-islemleri/vakif-istatistikleri/yeni-vakiflar) lists workforce and selected-data PDFs; the research tool could not decode their contents. No values have been transcribed from those files.

Next: inspect original tables; establish entity and workplace coverage; assess matching aggregate employee counts; select a compatible denominator and exact series ID; pilot a targeted survey for unresolved fields. Restricted data access, record linkage and institutional cooperation are not assumed. Any future collection must define lawful access, consent where applicable, disclosure control and storage arrangements before requesting records.

## International admission gate

[OECD research](https://www.oecd.org/content/dam/oecd/en/publications/reports/2024/06/insights-from-social-and-solidarity-economy-data_888cf72a/71d212f3-en.pdf) identifies definition and coverage differences. [ILO's 2026 update](https://www.ilo.org/resource/news/advancing-global-statistical-framework-social-and-solidarity-economy) describes ongoing statistical framework development. Neither establishes a verified annual country series for this pilot.

Admit an indicator only after documenting consistent entity perimeter, active status, paid-employment unit, geography, reference year, source vintage, duplication treatment and missingness across the chosen countries. Then review conceptual relevance, overlap, normalisation, weights and sensitivity. Until that review, publish research status and descriptive national findings separately from rankings.

## Release record

For every future observation retain indicator ID, geography, observation year, numerator, denominator, value, statistical unit, source table/series ID, exact URL, source version, retrieval date, coverage, quality flags, revision history and review decision. Preserve original extracts independently from calculations. Publish only aggregate, validated observations with a versioned methodology.
